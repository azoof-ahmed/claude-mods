# Successor startup: resume a lane from its card

Your role is in the line that launched you. Aim to read less than 25k tokens before you start working.

## Read (this is the whole list)
1. {{rules}}.
2. {{cards}}/<role>.md, your lane card.
   - If there is no card, look for the brief named on the bus or in {{reports}}/<role>_progress.md.
   - Write the card from it first. The template is {{cards}}/README.md.
3. Your backlog:
   - with the agent-bus plugin, `bus digest --as <role>`;
   - otherwise, the end of {{reports}}/<role>_progress.md.
4. Only the sections your card points to, by range.

## Then
- Arm the bus watch Monitor if the session context asks for it.
- Do the card's "Next 3 actions".
- Keep the card current. It is the next successor's handoff.
